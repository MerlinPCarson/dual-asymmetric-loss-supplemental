Balancing Speech Reconstruction and Noise Suppression Using Dual-Asymmetric Loss

Merlin Carson¹ Suyash Dandekar²

¹Skyworks Solutions, USA

²Georgia Institue of Technology, USA

merlin.carson@skyworksinc.com, sdandekar7@gatech.edu

Abstract

AI-based speech enhancement algorithms are prevalent in speech transmission applications and consumer audio devices. These algorithms typically utilize neural networks to predict filters or masks that attenuate noise in speech signals. However, due to the often-overlapping frequency responses of speech and noise, this process can degrade speech quality or result in residual noise artifacts. In this paper, we present a novel loss function with an adjustable parameter to balance the importance of reconstructing the original speech and the effectiveness of noise suppression. We demonstrate the efficacy of this loss with a variety of state-of-the-art architectures. Experimental results demonstrate improvements over baseline models for the targeted outcome: higher speech quality or enhanced noise suppression. Furthermore, the loss function exhibits a correlation of 0.95 for speech and -0.96 for noise metrics when tested across 13 distinct parameter values.
